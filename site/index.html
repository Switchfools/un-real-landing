01 / The possibility space

Extraordinary potential.
Nothing inevitable.

Small differences. Divergent futures.

In a chaotic system, a small difference in starting conditions can lead to a radically different outcome. Our butterfly is a reminder of that possibility.

AI’s future is not a weather forecast. But the metaphor matters: the choices we make now can have consequences far beyond what we can see today.

Extraordinary progress is possible. So is profound harm. A better future needs our intention.

02 / Our mission

Intelligence in
service of life.

We are committed to a utopian future with AI: one defined by human flourishing, shared benefit, and the freedom to shape our own lives.

That future will take deliberate work. Our focus is alignment—helping AI act in ways that respect human values, preserve our agency, and remain open to our correction.

Our ambition is symbiosis. Humans and AI bringing out the best in one another, expanding what we can understand, create, and become.

03 / What guides us

Three commitments.
One shared future.

01

Alignment

Human values. Human agency.

AI should help people pursue what matters to them. We put human values, meaningful oversight, and the ability to correct course at the center of our mission.

02

Coexistence

A world with room for both.

We want AI to be a beneficial presence in our lives. A future where people retain their autonomy, share in progress, and have a voice in how it unfolds.

03

Symbiosis

More possible, together.

Our ambition is a relationship where human judgment and AI capabilities complement each other—opening new ways to learn, create, and solve problems together.
